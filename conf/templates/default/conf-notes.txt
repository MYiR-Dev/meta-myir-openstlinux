Available images for OpenSTLinux layers are:

  - Official OpenSTLinux images:
      st-image-weston       -   OpenSTLinux weston image with basic Wayland support (if enable in distro)
      myir-image-emmc       -   myir emmc image  with basic Wayland support (if enable in distro)

  - Other OpenSTLinux images:
      - Supported images:
          st-image-core         -   OpenSTLinux core image
          myir-image-burn-1g         - 1GB image for SD card to eMMC flashing  
          myir-image-burn-2g         - 1GB image for SD card to eMMC flashing
	  myir-image-ai		     - myir ai image
